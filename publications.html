Technometrics · 2026 · Accepted

Spatio-temporal Gaussian process for building terrain-incorporating wind power curves

A. Chokhachian, V. R. Joseph, Y. Ding

Developed a terrain-aware spatio-temporal Gaussian process for large-scale wind farm data with asynchronous turbine signals, integrating distribution-preserving compression and Kronecker matrix–vector operations. The proposed model outperforms XGBoost and deep learning baselines, with demonstrated potential for significant industrial cost savings.

INFORMS Journal on Data Science · 2026 · Published

Fast Gaussian process approximations for autocorrelated data

A. Chokhachian, M. Katzfuss, Y. Ding

Scaled Vecchia, laGP, and TwinGP are leading scalable Gaussian process models, yet they suffer from temporal overfitting when applied to autocorrelated data. We introduce Monte Carlo–based mitigation strategies applicable across all three methods, substantially improving predictive uncertainty calibration and distributional reliability.
