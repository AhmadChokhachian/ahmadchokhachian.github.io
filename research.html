Research

Research

I develop data science methods that turn turbine and farm data into reliable performance estimates, calibrated uncertainty, and early warnings.

Projects

Terrain-aware wind power curves

Completed

A spatio-temporal Gaussian process that brings terrain into power curve estimation across a wind farm. It handles asynchronous turbine signals through distribution-preserving compression and Kronecker matrix–vector operations.

Technometrics, accepted

Scalable Gaussian processes for autocorrelated data

Completed

Scaled Vecchia, laGP and TwinGP overfit in time when data are autocorrelated. Monte Carlo–based mitigation strategies restore calibrated predictive uncertainty across all three.

INFORMS Journal on Data Science

Cross-farm power curves

Under review

A transfer-learning framework that adapts power curves to new farms with a supervised distributional similarity metric and Bayesian neural networks, reducing RMSE by 9–23%.

SSRN preprint

Uncertainty quantification for power curves

Ongoing

Information-adaptive residual pooling for estimating the prediction error of a fitted power curve, and a study of variance collapse in predictive uncertainty methods.

Two manuscripts in preparation

Agentic AI for turbine fault warning

Ongoing

Agentic AI frameworks that detect drivetrain and gearbox faults early from high-frequency vibration data.

Deep learning for wind farms

2026

A Bayesian neural network for irregular mixed static and dynamic power forecasting, and a graph neural network that models spatial interactions between turbines from the farm layout.

Software
