§1About

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Yu Ding.

My research develops statistical and machine learning methods for wind energy. I build Gaussian process models that scale to large, autocorrelated data. I design wind power curves that account for terrain and transfer across wind farms. I also develop agentic AI systems for early fault warning in wind turbines.
