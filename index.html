Ahmadreza (Ahmad) Chokhachian

PhD Candidate in Machine Learning
H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Yu Ding. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My research develops statistical and machine learning methods for wind energy. I build Gaussian process models that scale to large, autocorrelated data. I design wind power curves that account for terrain and transfer across wind farms. I also study uncertainty quantification and agentic AI systems for turbine fault warning.
